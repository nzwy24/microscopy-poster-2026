Live-cell Imaging of EGFP-Cc.Cdc24 Dynamics

Time-lapse observations of Cdc24 localization during hyphal growth and mitosis in Coprinopsis cinerea.

1. EGFP-Cc.Cdc24 Monokaryon

1-1

Tip region

Description

Although the fluorescence signal is faint, EGFP-Cc.Cdc24 localization can be observed at the hyphal tip.

1-2

Mitosis 1

1-3

Mitosis 2

Description · Mitosis 1 & 2

During mitosis, punctate EGFP-Cc.Cdc24 signals are observed in association with nuclei. After nuclear division, localization is observed near the dolipore septum, in a region tentatively interpreted as the parenthesome.

2. EGFP-Cc.Cdc24 / mCherry-Cc.α-Tubulin Co-expression strain

2-1

EGFP-Cc.Cdc24

Description

Hyphal tip localization was observed in the co-expression strain, as in the strain expressing EGFP-Cc.Cdc24 alone. In contrast, transient punctate signals associated with nuclei were not detected. This suggests the possibility that such transient signals may occur only during mitosis accompanied by septum formation.

2-2

mCherry-Cc.α-Tubulin

Description

During mitosis, bright filamentous localization of mCherry-Cc.α-Tubulin was observed.
